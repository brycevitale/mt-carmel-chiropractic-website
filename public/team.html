Meet the Team

Dr. Colleen Pace, DC, and Dr. Michael Pace, DC Mt Carmel Chiropractic & Acupuncture — Hamden, Connecticut

Dr. Colleen Pace, DC

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Colleen V. Pace, DC, graduated from Palmer College of Chiropractic in 1998 and has served the Hamden community for 25 years as a chiropractor and craniosacral therapist. She is certified in the Webster Technique for pregnancy and completed certification in Regenerative Orthopedic and Infusion Care through the University of Bridgeport in 2025. She currently serves as a full-time clinician at the University of Bridgeport’s chiropractic school, a position she has held since 2023.

Dr. Michael Pace, DC

Doctor of Chiropractic

Dr. Michael Pace graduated from Palmer College of Chiropractic in June 2000 and completed his fellowship in acupuncture in 2002. With over 25 years of private practice experience, he focuses on treating musculoskeletal conditions in patients of all ages. His approach incorporates acupuncture, dry needling, shockwave therapy, and other treatment methods. He also works with attorneys representing individuals injured in workplace and automobile accidents.

Outside of his practice, Dr. Pace holds a U.S. Coast Guard 25-Ton Master’s License and has served as an operator with Thimble Island Cruises for the past four years.

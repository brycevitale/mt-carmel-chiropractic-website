Physical Therapy in Hamden, CT

Movement-focused care centered on strength, mobility, and rehabilitation for everyday activities.

About physical therapy

Physical therapy generally centers on how the body moves during daily activities, work, and recreation, and on building strength and mobility over time.

Why people seek an evaluation

People may seek an evaluation for concerns such as:

  • Sports injuries
  • Joint discomfort
  • Limits in daily activities

Whether this type of care is appropriate depends on your individual situation and a professional assessment.

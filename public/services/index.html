Explore our services

Chiropractic Care

The spine, joints, and how you move

Chiropractic care focuses on the spine, joints, and the muscles that support them. Patients often ask about it for back pain, neck pain, stiffness, or discomfort that affects everyday movement.

Read more about chiropractic care

Acupuncture

Very fine needles at selected points

Acupuncture places very fine needles at selected points on the body. Some people explore it alongside other care for concerns such as chronic pain, neck discomfort, or headaches.

Read more about acupuncture

Cupping Therapy

Suction cups placed on the skin

Cupping uses specially designed cups that create suction on the skin. Some people use it for muscle tension. It can leave round marks that fade over time, and evidence for some claimed benefits is limited.

Read more about cupping therapy

Shockwave Therapy

Targeted acoustic waves

Shockwave therapy delivers targeted acoustic waves and may be considered for certain long-lasting tendon or soft-tissue concerns, such as heel pain or plantar fasciitis.

Read more about shockwave therapy

Physical Therapy

Movement, strength, and mobility

Physical therapy focuses on movement, strength, mobility, and rehabilitation. People may seek an evaluation for sports injuries, joint discomfort, or limits in daily activities.

Read more about physical therapy

Craniosacral Therapy

A gentle, light-touch approach

Craniosacral therapy uses very light touch, and some patients seek it for relaxation or tension-related discomfort. Evidence for treating specific medical conditions is limited.

Read more about craniosacral therapy
